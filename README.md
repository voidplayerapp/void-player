# VOID Player

Live TV, movies and series from your own playlists, with VOID Connect, VDownloader and Telegram built in. One app for
Android TV and Android phones.

**Closed beta.** Downloads are open to everyone; using the app needs a personal access code.
To request one, write to [voidplayer@outlook.de](mailto:voidplayer@outlook.de?subject=VOID%20Player%20Beta).

[Deutsche Fassung](README.de.md) · [Website](https://voidplayerapp.github.io)

## Highlights

- **VOID Connect:** your own library with posters, built from your sources, without any external server. Write a
  source yourself, let an AI assistant draft it, or add a finished one with a single link.
- **VDownloader:** just give VOID links, including RAR archives. It downloads, unpacks automatically and saves straight
  to your TV, a USB drive or a NAS, and you can start watching while it is still unpacking.
- **Telegram:** play videos from your own chats and channels on your TV or phone, right next to the rest of your
  library.
- **Private by design:** no account, no tracking. Logins stay on your devices and only go to the service they belong
  to. Your devices sync directly over your home network, encrypted, with no cloud in between.
- Live TV with a timeline guide, recordings, sports, profiles with a kids profile and Stremio-compatible add-ons.

## Download

The latest version is always under [Releases](../../releases).

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `VOID-Player-TV.apk` | Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV |
| `VOID-Player-Phone.apk` | Android phones |
| `VOID-Player-Universal.apk` | Any ARM device (larger file) |

On a TV, open the Downloader app and enter `voidplayerapp.github.io/tv`.

## Install

1. Download the file for your device.
2. Allow the installation when Android asks.
3. Open VOID Player and enter your access code.

## Updates

VOID Player checks for new versions itself. Android asks once for confirmation, then the update installs.

## Good to know

VOID Player doesn't come with channels, movies or playlists. Use it with content you have the rights to.
Free during the beta. No ads, no tracking, no account.
