# VOID Player

**No account. No tracking. Sync without the cloud.**

Live TV, movies and series from your own sources, on Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV and Android phones.
VOID Connect, VDownloader, Telegram and Stremio-compatible add-ons live in the same app.

[Website](https://voidplayerapp.github.io/) · [Downloads](https://github.com/voidplayerapp/void-player/releases) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/7EcNAesKW) · [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Voidplayer/) · [Deutsch](README.de.md)

**Closed beta.** Anyone can download the APKs. Using the app needs a personal access code.
Ask in **#beta-access** on [Discord](https://discord.gg/7EcNAesKW), or email
[voidplayer@outlook.de](mailto:voidplayer@outlook.de?subject=VOID%20Player%20Beta).
Just send a short message saying you'd like to try VOID. Requests are private and handled personally.

## What you can do

| Area | In VOID Player |
|---|---|
| Live TV | Xtream and M3U sources, a timeline guide, catch-up where your provider supports it, and recordings on TV. |
| Movies and series | Posters, descriptions and cast for your library, plus favorites and playback history. |
| VOID Connect | A library built from your sources, with posters and playback inside VOID. |
| Add-ons | Add Stremio-compatible add-ons by link and browse their catalogs in the app. |
| VDownloader | Download links and RAR archives, unpack them and save to TV, USB or NAS. Supported downloads can play while unpacking. |
| Telegram | Play videos from your own chats and channels on TV or phone. |
| At home | Profiles, a kids profile and encrypted sync directly between your devices over your home network. |

Xtream, M3U, SMB and local media are supported sources. TV playback uses Android's Media3 player, phone playback uses mpv.
Format support, HDR and audio passthrough depend on your device and source.

## Getting started

1. Request your beta code, then download the APK for your device.
2. Open the file and allow installation when Android asks.
3. Open VOID Player, enter your code and add your own sources.

New releases can be offered inside the app. Android asks for confirmation before installation.
You can also download a newer APK from Releases and install it over the existing app. Keep your app data.
